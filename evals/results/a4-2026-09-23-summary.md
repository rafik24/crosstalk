## Finding
haiku RELIABLY loads the skill and names itself (`cc-name`), and obeys the silent-re-arm contract when
the task is framed around arming. Its GAP is the always-listen rule: it did NOT arm the `cc-ws` receiver
in 2 of 3 cases. In handoff-ack that cascades — no receiver → the peer handoff is never delivered → no
`cc-ack`. Bus logs confirm: in every case only the hook's default `cwd-*` id is registered; no case shows
a live named receiver beyond the silent-rearm case. So the single most safety-relevant contract (arm
receive before editing; ack a handoff) is the least reliably followed by haiku.

Caveats: n=1 per case; silent-rearm hit maxTurns (8) so its transcript is truncated but graders passed;
per-run traces were not saved (tracePath empty) — grader + bus-log evidence only.
